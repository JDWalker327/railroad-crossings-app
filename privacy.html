Privacy Policy for Railroad Crossings

Effective date: June 16, 2026

Railroad Crossings ("the app") provides railroad crossing-related information and tools. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may collect, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information.

We may collect limited technical information necessary to operate and improve the app. Depending on the version of the app and the services used, this may include:

  • Device and app performance information
  • Crash logs and diagnostic information
  • Basic usage analytics
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting support

Location Information

If the app provides location-based features, it may request access to your device location only to provide those features. Location information is used only as needed for app functionality.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate and maintain the app
  • Improve performance, reliability, and features
  • Diagnose crashes and technical problems
  • Respond to support requests

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help operate the app, such as hosting, analytics, crash reporting, or payment/subscription services, only as necessary to provide the app.

Subscriptions and Payments

If the app offers subscriptions or in-app purchases, payment processing is handled by the platform provider (such as Google Play or Apple). We do not receive or store your full payment card information.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to operate the app, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Your Choices

You can limit certain data collection by adjusting your device settings, such as location permissions, or by discontinuing use of the app.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted at this page with a revised effective date.
